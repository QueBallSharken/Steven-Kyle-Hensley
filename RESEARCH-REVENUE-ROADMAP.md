No client engagement should imply:

- OWASP endorsement

- certification

# 17. WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT

The commercial proposition is NOT:

"I know cybersecurity."

The proposition is:

"I investigate whether governance claims actually survive the point where
systems change state."

The research provides a specialized analytical lens.

Relevant questions include:

Where does authority exist?

Where is it re-established?

Where can it disappear?

Where can it be substituted?

Where can it become stale?

Where can a fallback bypass it?

Where is the actual irreversible mutation?

What evidence proves the decision?

Can the system still refuse?

# 24. RESEARCH INTEGRITY RULE

Money does not determine whether a technical claim is true.

A paying customer does not become evidence.

A sponsor does not become an authority.

A GitHub star does not become validation.

A successful prototype does not automatically establish a security guarantee.

The research standard remains:

PRESERVE THE RECORD.
PRESERVE THE EVIDENCE.
TEST THE CLAIM.
DOCUMENT THE LIMITATION.
LET THE RESULT SPEAK.
