IMPORTANT IP RULE
=================

Do not publicly disclose additional implementation details
merely because they sound patent-worthy.

Before making new public disclosures concerning potentially
patentable mechanisms:

1. Preserve the existing public record.
2. Identify the potentially novel mechanisms.
3. Have qualified patent counsel perform a prior-art analysis.
4. Determine what can actually be protected.
5. Decide whether protection should be:
   - patent
   - trade secret
   - copyright
   - open-source publication
   - defensive publication
6. Only then choose the disclosure strategy.
